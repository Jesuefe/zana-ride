Zanaride Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zanaride (also branded as Zana), the mobility and delivery application previously listed as Showmine TV, collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the app and its related services.

1. App and developer identification

App name: Zanaride (Zana)

Google Play developer account name: Showmine tecnologies.

Operating company: ZANA RIDE INC LTD.

ZANA RIDE INC LTD operates the Zanaride service. The Google Play developer profile for the existing app listing displays the developer name “Showmine tecnologies”. This policy applies to the Zanaride app distributed through that existing listing, including versions that may still display the former name Showmine TV.

Privacy contact: support@zanaride.rw

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use and your role in the service, we may collect:

  • Account and contact information: name, phone number, email address, profile details and account credentials.
  • Location information: precise or approximate device location, pickup and drop-off points, delivery addresses and trip routes when needed to match, provide or support rides and deliveries. You can control device location permissions in your device settings; some location-based features may not work without them.
  • Service and transaction information: ride and delivery requests, order details, receipts, fares, payment status, wallet activity and related support records.
  • Communications: messages, support requests, reports and information you choose to provide when contacting us or another service participant.
  • Driver and merchant onboarding information: where applicable, identity, vehicle, business and verification details, including documents submitted for review.
  • Device and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, diagnostic data, log information and interactions needed to maintain and secure the service.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts; connect customers with drivers, merchants and delivery partners; arrange and track rides and deliveries; process payments and wallet transactions; provide customer support; verify drivers and merchants; prevent fraud and protect users; maintain, troubleshoot and improve the app; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Location permissions

Zanaride uses location information to support pickup and drop-off selection, nearby driver or merchant matching, route progress and delivery fulfilment. Location may be collected while you use relevant app features. Background location is used only if a feature requires it and you have granted the relevant device permission.

5. How information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant information:

  • with drivers, customers, merchants or delivery partners when necessary to fulfil a ride, order or delivery;
  • with payment providers, including Paypack, to process and reconcile transactions;
  • with hosting, security, communications, analytics and technical service providers that help operate the service;
  • when required by law, legal process or a valid request from a competent authority; or
  • to protect users, investigate fraud, enforce our terms or protect the rights and safety of Zanaride and others.

We limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements and protect the service. Retention periods vary by information type and purpose. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You can manage device permissions, including location access, through your device settings. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal, safety and record-keeping requirements. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children's privacy

Zanaride is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app, services or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date above.
