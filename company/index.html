A builder's approach to Rwanda's growing economy.

Ogbiti Davis Kalu founded Bastion Holdings with a simple conviction: Kigali's fast-growing economy needed infrastructure built specifically for it, not adapted from elsewhere. That conviction became Zana Ride — a ride-hailing, delivery, and marketplace platform designed around how people in Kigali actually move, shop, and get paid.

Before Zana Ride, his work centered on currency exchange, giving him a close, practical view of how money actually moves through Rwanda's economy — cash, mobile money, and digital payments, often within the same transaction. That grounding shapes how Bastion Holdings builds today: financial systems designed for the reality on the ground, not assumptions imported from other markets.

Under his leadership, Bastion Holdings operates with a straightforward philosophy — build for the specific market in front of you, move deliberately, and keep the people actually using the platform, drivers, riders, and merchants alike, at the center of every decision.
